## Projects

### Bliss Hour Events (in progress)
A website for my brother's photo booth business, built from scratch.

- **Tech:** React, TypeScript, Sass, Vite
- **My role:** solo developer. I handle the design, the code and the client conversations. My brother gives me free rein on the layout and how things are displayed, so it's been a fun creative project, and he's liked the ideas I've come up with.
- **What I learned:** most of the work is in the small CSS details. The gallery looked fine on my laptop, but on my phone the photos overlapped and the gaps were uneven. Sorting it out took a whole afternoon, and it finally made flexbox and grid click for me.

### AFW (group project, Dev Academy)
A Wordle-inspired game where the word length changes each round, built with a team in one week and presented. Scores go on a leaderboard.

- **Tech:** React, TypeScript, SQLite, Auth0, Tailwind CSS
- **My role:** front-end functionality, game logic, client-side tests, the Give Up button that reveals the word, sound effects and the styling. I was also our official Vibes Watcher: I ran stand-ups and retros and organised games and breaks to keep the team connected.
- **The hard part:** I knew exactly what the game should do, but turning that into code was a different problem. Checking a guess against the word and deciding when you've won took me far longer than I expected.
- **What I learned:** teamwork is hard until you find a way of working that suits everyone. We had conflicting ideas early on, and what helped was keeping our retros small and stress-free so they never felt like a massive thing. Once we had that system, I enjoyed the project a lot more.
